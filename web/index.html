Why these games are playable here

Each game's rules live in a small library with no engine dependency — snake-lib, tic-tac-toe-lib and breakout-lib — and each engine gets a thin frontend over it. Tic-tac-toe runs on four of them (a terminal, an ASCII console, Bevy and Godot); Snake on two; Breakout proves the pattern survives continuous physics. None of the frontends contains a rule of the game, which is what makes compiling one of them to WebAssembly a build-target change rather than a rewrite.
